create-eth now lives in the [scaffold-eth-2](https://github.com/scaffold-eth/scaffold-eth-2) repository. The `npx create-eth@latest` command and the generated projects work the same way.

Breaking for project code: the deprecated string forms of `useScaffoldWriteContract` and `useDeployedContractInfo` are removed (https://github.com/scaffold-eth/scaffold-eth-2/pull/1333).
